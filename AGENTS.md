## Default educational experience criteria

Apply these user-defined criteria to all future experiments:

- Classroom purpose first: choose explicit learning objectives. For primary and secondary school, align with the relevant textbook lesson. An experiment supplements a selected part of the lesson; it need not cover the entire lesson. Balance educational value and appeal.
- Independent discovery: let learners choose, predict, investigate, observe, compare, and draw their own conclusions. Avoid revealing explanations before investigation. Encourage curiosity and further exploration; contextual hints may support learners without taking over.
- Purposeful challenges: offer optional, age-appropriate problems that deepen the selected learning objectives and develop problem solving. Let learners retry and experience achievement through their own discoveries.
- Real-life application: connect observations to concrete everyday situations and practical uses, and provide a bridge to hands-on classroom exploration.

For the Vietnamese preschool audience (ages 3–6), provide Vietnamese speech and instructions, simple actions, observable cause and effect, and age-appropriate scaffolding. Check scientific accuracy and interaction realism before adding decorative rewards. Reviews should distinguish direct observations from assumptions about children's responses; actual child usability testing is needed to validate those assumptions.
