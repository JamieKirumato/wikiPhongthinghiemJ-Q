Mappings: mining impacts for stone on tile; light glass impacts for tank walls;
light plate cracks for egg shell; soft impacts for fruit and sand contact.
Three variants per material avoid mechanically repeating the same sound.
Water splash remains the existing procedural WAV; Web Audio is an error fallback.
